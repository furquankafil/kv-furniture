About KV Furniture

Built on Craft.
Guided by Trust.

A premium, factory-direct bespoke furniture manufacturer from Kirti Nagar, Delhi — creating highly customised furniture for clients who care about design, craftsmanship, materials and transparency.

The KV Philosophy

Made once. Made yours.

KV Furniture began in May 2022 with a simple conviction — that furniture should be built around the family that lives with it. Not stocked, not standardised, not hurried.

Every piece leaves our Kirti Nagar factory only after it has been measured, discussed, material-matched and inspected for one specific home. That is slower than retail. It is also why our pieces feel different the moment you sit in them.
